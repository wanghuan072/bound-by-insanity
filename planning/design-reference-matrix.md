## Two Composition Directions

A — full-width dramatic cover above the player: strong game recognition, but the near-square cover plus intro creates excessive height. It relegates the actual action and detailed reading; overlay text competes with the illustrated faces/title. Reject for player priority and natural image ratio.

B — ink journal with a focused player and compact main-game reading rail: real cover is completely visible inside a wide player shell, action is centred away from the lower title, while three actual article entry cards clarify the next task. Body remains full left-column width; the rail ends naturally without forcing body underneath it. Select B. Distinct surfaces, narrow crimson ink strokes and a broken-heart wordmark echo the actual dialogue paper and warning palette.

Neither direction uses unrelated gaming stock, a neon room, random gradients, copied layouts or oversized H1s.
