# Bound By Insanity

Bound By Insanity is a dark, illustrated visual novel demo about a defined young woman and her encounters with Miles Falken and Adrien Lockhart. We read dialogue and narration, notice shifts in expression and context, and occasionally choose a reply. The story is intended for adults and includes disturbing relationships and fictional violence.

This is an independent fan website at **https://boundbyinsanity.org**. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to the original game website or its creator.

## What makes the demo distinctive

The artwork moves between rough monochrome drawings, torn-paper compositions, close-ups and fuller colored scenes. A portrait can convey a mood, but we learn more by reading it alongside the words and circumstances of an encounter. Bound By Insanity gives us reasons to slow down rather than race through its pictures.

The protagonist has a defined presence in the story. Miles and Adrien are central to how we interpret its relationships, but the demo does not offer a complete collection of finished character endings. We keep what we can play today separate from plans for the full game.

## Reading and replay guidance

We begin by reading the adult-content warning and choosing a comfortable pace. Let a passage finish appearing before advancing. A long paragraph can continue to expand before its heart-shaped completion cue appears.

If we miss a line, History lets us inspect previous dialogue, while upward-wheel rollback can return us to an earlier passage in the demonstrated browser version. These functions serve different purposes: reviewing text does not automatically replay a decision. The guidance beside the player explains their limits and practical uses.

Before comparing a reply, we can use the game’s save interface and check the slot we intend to load. An immediate response may change without producing a different route ending. Saves belong to the game and browser environment; older saves may not work with a revised demo, and progress is not guaranteed to transfer between devices.

## Common questions

### Is this the complete game?

No. The material discussed here is a visual novel demo in development. The Endings page distinguishes its shared Act 1 content from the four planned major routes, two associated with each central character. Planned routes are not presented as currently unlockable endings.

### Do all replies lead to different endings?

A reply can alter an exchange without changing the demo’s larger story boundary. We can compare the visible result of a choice without treating every changed line as a separate completed route. The Endings page explains a specific replay example.

### How do we open the game?

Choose Play Now on the Home page. The game loads in a third-party iframe only after that action. Webpage Fullscreen and Browser Fullscreen offer different ways to enlarge the player. If loading fails or times out, use the visible recovery control; the article remains readable independently of the game window.

### Will browser saves always survive?

No. Storage behavior depends on the game, browser settings and version. Private browsing, clearing site data or changing device can affect progress. This fan website does not store a backup of a player’s game save.

### Can we read on a phone or tablet?

The site adapts to tablet and mobile widths, with a hamburger menu at 1024px and below and a compact single-column layout at 768px and below. Embedded game compatibility can differ from the written pages, so use the available reading guidance when a third-party player is unavailable.

### How do we report a problem?

Visit [Contact Us](https://boundbyinsanity.org/contact) and use the plain-text address **support@boundbyinsanity.org**. Include the affected page, your browser and device, and the specific text or control involved. Do not send passwords, payment information or private save files.
